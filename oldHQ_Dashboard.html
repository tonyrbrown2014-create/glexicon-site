Gatekeeper Architecture

Gatekeeper nodes sit between your Master Database and your Satellite Nodes. Every request a Satellite Node forwards is checked by a Gatekeeper for relevance before it ever reaches the Master Database — an extra ring of verification around your core data.

Gatekeeper calibration is a technical configuration handled by your IT / engineering staff and is not adjustable from this dashboard.

Quick Start Guide

A short walkthrough of what each section of this dashboard does.

Deployment Command
Your home page. Fleet-wide key quota, node heartbeat, and overall system status at a glance.
Generate Tokens
Convert primary keys, foreign keys, or a CSV column into unique Glexicon tokens. Draws from your Tick Tock Points allocation.
Node Registry
Inspect, manage, and decommission individual Deployment Nodes across your fleet.
Protocol Analytics
Throughput and storage reduction figures, broken down by node tier and by site.
Key Provisioning
Issue new cryptographic Leaf Keys to bring a new Deployment Node online.
Audit & Verification
The immutable log of every heartbeat, key event, and token generation across your fleet.
Account Settings
Your licence profile, key quota, and security configuration.
Note: This guide covers dashboard navigation only. For protocol documentation or account support, use the contact details on your licence agreement.
